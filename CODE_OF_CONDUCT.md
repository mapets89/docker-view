# Code of Conduct

We are committed to a professional, respectful, harassment-free project environment. Be considerate, assume good intent while evaluating technical claims on evidence, respect privacy, and do not disclose security-sensitive information publicly.

Unacceptable conduct includes harassment, discrimination, threats, unwanted sexual attention, sustained disruption, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation when conduct threatens the project or its participants.

Although external contributions are currently closed, this policy applies to every project space. Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainers. Reports will be reviewed fairly and confidentially to the extent possible.
